## Contradiction review

No material contradiction affected the published boundaries. Source-specific diagnosis and treatment detail was intentionally excluded. The dog digestive source was intermittently unavailable through the research reader; only cross-source, non-diagnostic escalation wording was retained for the dog rows.

## Private provenance

The seven publisher/title/URL records and nine row mappings are retained in `guidance_sources` plus `concern_guidance.source_id`. Retrieval date for this fixed version: 2026-09-04. Public application code does not select or render these records.
